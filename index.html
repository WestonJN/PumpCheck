    Questions about fuel prices

    When do South African fuel prices change?

    Petrol and diesel prices are adjusted on the first Wednesday of every month. The Department of Mineral and Petroleum Resources announces the change a few days earlier. Pump Check switches to the new prices on the day they take effect.

    Why are diesel prices shown as wholesale?

    The government sets the wholesale diesel price only. Garages add their own margin, so the pump price differs from station to station. Add your garage's extra rand per litre in the calculator to match your receipt.

    How does Pump Check work out my fuel cost?

    It multiplies the litres you use by the price per litre. Litres come from your distance and your vehicle's fuel use in litres per 100 km. Each figure also shows the price from the previous adjustment, so you can see what the increase costs you.

    What is the difference between inland and coastal prices?

    Fuel is cheaper at the coast because it costs less to transport. Gauteng and other inland areas pay the inland price. Petrol 93 is sold inland only.
